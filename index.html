FAQ

Common questions about PPC management.

What is PPC Management, and why is it important for Amazon sellers?

PPC (Pay-Per-Click) Management is about creating, optimizing, and scaling ad campaigns to maximize ROI while minimizing wasted spend. It ensures your ads drive consistent sales, lower ACOS, and boost overall profitability on Amazon.

How does SellMora manage PPC campaigns?

We handle every aspect, including competitor analysis, keyword research, bid optimization, and ad performance monitoring. Our goal is to create campaigns that deliver high conversion rates while keeping your ACOS and TACOS in check.

What’s included in the “Build Your Winning PPC Strategy” phase?

We start by analyzing your current campaigns, assessing ACOS and TACOS, and performing keyword research. We also analyze competitor strategies to design ad campaigns tailored to maximize your conversion rates.

How do you optimize PPC campaigns for maximum ROI?

We continuously adjust bids, refine ad creatives, and monitor market trends to ensure your campaigns are competitive and profitable. Our aim is to lower ACOS and TACOS while driving consistent sales.

Can you help reduce my ACOS?

Absolutely! Our strategies are designed to achieve a lower ACOS, typically targeting 10% or less, while ensuring your campaigns remain highly effective.

What does TACOS mean, and why is it important?

TACOS (Total Advertising Cost of Sales) measures ad spend as a percentage of total sales. It’s a critical metric for understanding the overall profitability of your campaigns and ensuring long-term growth.

Do you provide reports on campaign performance?

Yes, we deliver detailed monthly reports with actionable insights. These reports help track progress, optimize strategies, and keep your campaigns profitable.

How do you monitor competitor strategies?

We use advanced tools and market research to analyze competitors’ ads, keywords, and trends

What is catalog management, and why is it important for my business?

Catalog management is the process of organizing, optimizing, and maintaining your product listings to ensure they are accurate, visible, and compelling.

What services does SellMora offer under catalog management?

Which marketplaces can you help with?

We work across Amazon's North American and European marketplaces, including the U.S., UK, Germany and France.

Will my reviews carry over?

Amazon shares reviews between some marketplaces for the same product. We set up your listings so you benefit wherever that applies.

Do my listings need translating?

Yes. We localize titles, bullets and keywords for each market, because direct translation rarely matches how locals search.

May 10, 2025 · Amazon seller tips

How to get to the top of the page on Amazon: do's & don'ts

The issues sellers face most, and what it really takes to land at the top of Amazon search.

Why the top of the page matters

Most shoppers never scroll far. Listings on the first rows of search results collect the majority of clicks, so ranking higher is often the single biggest lever for organic sales.

Do: match what shoppers search

Start with keyword research. Put your most important search term early in the title, cover related terms in bullets and use the backend search fields for the rest. Write for people first; stuffing keywords hurts conversion.

Do: win the click and the sale

Amazon rewards listings that convert. A clear main image, a competitive price, strong reviews and a gallery that answers questions all push conversion up, and rankings follow.

Don't: rely on organic alone

New listings rarely rank without help. Sponsored Products campaigns on your core keywords build the sales velocity that tells Amazon your product deserves a higher spot.

Don't: ignore listing health

Suppressed listings, broken variations and stock-outs can wipe out rankings overnight. Check your catalogue regularly and keep inventory steady.

May 10, 2025 · Amazon seller tips

Targeting the right audience with Amazon CPM ad types

How Amazon's CPM ad types help you reach the shoppers who matter most.

What CPM means

CPM stands for cost per mille: you pay per thousand impressions instead of per click. It suits goals like awareness and reaching shoppers before they search for your product.

Sponsored Display

Sponsored Display can run on a viewable-impression basis to reach audiences on and off Amazon, including shoppers who viewed your products or similar ones.

Sponsored Brands video

Video placements in search results grab attention and tell your brand story in seconds, which is ideal for products that need to be seen in use.

Choosing the right audience

Start with remarketing to people who viewed your listings, then test in-market and lifestyle audiences. Measure new-to-brand sales, not just ACoS.

When to use CPM

Use CPM once your listings convert well. Paying for impressions only makes sense when the page they land on is ready to sell.

Why Amazon account management is crucial for FBA sellers

New to FBA? Here's what account management covers and why it matters.

What FBA sellers are

Fulfilled by Amazon (FBA) sellers send inventory to Amazon warehouses. Amazon stores, picks, packs, ships and handles returns, and your products become Prime-eligible.

Why management still matters

FBA handles logistics, not strategy. Someone still has to keep listings healthy, manage ads, plan inventory, watch fees and respond to account issues.

Account health

Policy warnings, late responses and listing violations can put your account at risk. Regular monitoring catches problems before they become suspensions.

Inventory planning

Running out of stock kills rankings, and overstocking adds storage fees. Good account management balances both using sales data.

Growing with confidence

With the operations under control, you can focus on new products and new markets, knowing the day-to-day is handled.
