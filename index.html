Common questions about PPC management.

What is PPC Management, and why is it important for Amazon sellers?

PPC (Pay-Per-Click) Management is about creating, optimizing, and scaling ad campaigns to maximize ROI while minimizing wasted spend. It ensures your ads drive consistent sales, lower ACOS, and boost overall profitability on Amazon.

How does SellMora manage PPC campaigns?

We handle every aspect, including competitor analysis, keyword research, bid optimization, and ad performance monitoring. Our goal is to create campaigns that deliver high conversion rates while keeping your ACOS and TACOS in check.

What’s included in the “Build Your Winning PPC Strategy” phase?

We start by analyzing your current campaigns, assessing ACOS and TACOS, and performing keyword research. We also analyze competitor strategies to design ad campaigns tailored to maximize your conversion rates.

How do you optimize PPC campaigns for maximum ROI?

We continuously adjust bids, refine ad creatives, and monitor market trends to ensure your campaigns are competitive and profitable. Our aim is to lower ACOS and TACOS while driving consistent sales.

Can you help reduce my ACOS?

Absolutely! Our strategies are designed to achieve a lower ACOS, typically targeting 10% or less, while ensuring your campaigns remain highly effective.

What does TACOS mean, and why is it important?

TACOS (Total Advertising Cost of Sales) measures ad spend as a percentage of total sales. It’s a critical metric for understanding the overall profitability of your campaigns and ensuring long-term growth.

Do you provide reports on campaign performance?

Yes, we deliver detailed monthly reports with actionable insights. These reports help track progress, optimize strategies, and keep your campaigns profitable.

How do you monitor competitor strategies?

How to Get to the Top of the Page on Amazon: Do’s & Don’ts

So, if you are an Amazon seller and the same query is ticking through your head, no need to worry because you are in the right spot now. In this blog post, we will uncover some expert tips on how to rank on the top page on Amazon. Let’s dive into the journey and learn together.

2025 and Amazon Algorithms

However, for 2025, there are way more predictions and expectations.

Understanding the A9 Algorithm

The A9 algorithm serves as the backbone of Amazon’s product search algorithm. It determines which product will appear for which customer. The goal is to analyze each product in detail to discover previous metrics. Relevant results are taken and then delivered to the customers accordingly.

In essence, the Amazon A9 algorithm provides a higher conversation rate with relevancy to the search query. Even A9 states that they analyze and observe past traffic patterns to decide the catalogue for customers.

Amazon SEO

Amazon’s SEO is a bit different from Google’s SEO. Amazon SEO is all about on-page SEO tactics, especially keyword integration. Amazon’s SEO ranking and conversation rate are based on more sales.

More sales = more money to be at the top of the Best Seller’s rank. Each category on Amazon has its own taxonomy of BSR. The lower the BSR, the higher the sales. Amazon SEO aligns with key factors, including relevancy, customer satisfaction, sales performance, etc.

Keyword Relevancy & Optimization

One of the best tactics for ranking on Amazon is relevance in keywords. The key is targeting the right keyword in the right place. Choosing the right specific keyword can control your purchase rate.

In Amazon keyword SEO, there are two options; the first is the short-term keywords, and the second category is the long-term keywords. Short-term keywords provide relevancy, but they are general. However, long-term keywords are more specific and give research competency.

Incorporate the keywords in your product’s title, description, product features, etc., to boost Amazon ranking and enhance product visibility.

  • The title: The title is essential to an Amazon product listing because every word is researchable. Amazon provides a 200-character limit for the title, so utilize it wisely and incorporate the keywords with ranking parameters.
  • Product features: When you want to know the details of the product, move to the product features section. Be mindful and mention the details in bullet points. Each bullet point has a limit of 500 characters, but try to make it clear and concise, and the 300-character limit is fair enough.
  • Description: Another area to focus on is the description portion, which requires entering a relevant description with keywords. The character limit is 2000 characters.
  • Image SEO: One more thing to keep in mind is Image SEO or image optimization. A high-quality image with a detailed description and A+ content enhances the customer’s engagement. First-class and fine images with clarity, higher resolution, and mobile-friendliness are your ultimatum to increase sales on Amazon.

Online reviews cultivate trust and credibility. Your first introduction is through your product; the second is your customer experience and product review. But make sure that your social proof doesn’t sound fake because Amazon’s algorithm heavily considers customer feedback.

Being moderate is the key. Strong seller feedback brings long-term success and is essential for elevating your performance on Amazon. Think about what the customer demands after ordering something.

Targeting the Right Audience with Amazon CPM Ad Types

Learn how Amazon CPM advertising works and how to target the right audience for more effective campaigns.

Digital advertising is a complex yet effective method of targeting the right audience. And one of the most efficient methods of digital advertising that Amazon shared with us is the Amazon CPM Ad. Amazon shared several Ad types with us, but according to me, the CPM Ad type stands out, and in this blog post, we will share with you the reason behind it.

First, we will learn what a CPM Ad type is, how it is different and efficient from other Ad types, and some tips that you can adopt to target your desired audience with CPM Ads. So, stay tuned to find out the secret behind CPM Ad type.

Table of Contents

  • Understanding What Amazon CPM Ad Types Are?
  • The Importance of Audience Targeting in Amazon CPM Campaigns
  • Types of Audience Targeting in Amazon CPM Ads
  • Best Practices for Targeting the Right Audience
  • Limitations & How to Overcome Them
  • Conclusion

Understanding What Amazon CPM Ad Types Are?

Okay, you understand that Amazon CPM Ads are a very effective method of targeting the right audience, but you might be wondering, what is Amazon CPM? Let me answer this question of yours. So, Amazon CPM, in its full form, is the cost per mile and cost per thousand impressions.

CPM is a pricing model in advertising and marketing . Alao calls it cost per thousand impressions, which means the total ad spend for every one thousand (1000) impressions an ad receives. CPM is a bit different from CPC (cost-per-click). A very brief difference is that CPM is a pricing model in which you pay a specific amount for 1000 impressions, while the CPC model is based on the number of clicks.

Numerous ad types use the CPM model, including Amazon Display Ads, Sponsored Display Ads, Amazon DSP, and Video Ads. The method of displaying ads is a bit different, but the concept is the same. The advertisers pay for every 100 impressions of their ad being viewed.

The Importance of Audience Targeting in Amazon CPM Campaigns

One would be called a fool if he or she asked the reason behind targeting the audience. Duhh, it’s obvious that you are at Amazon for selling, and selling can be easy if you target the right audience, and for that, you need a strategic approach.

Right audience targeting can enhance your brand’s visibility and create effectiveness in the ad-running process. These ads help build engagement visibility by offering retargeting options and visually appealing product promotions.

Another amazing thing about Amazon CPM is that one needs to pay after 1000 impressions, which is also cost-friendly. Let’s look at an example to understand better. Suppose you are displaying an ad, and you choose the CPM model.

The CPM rate is $5 per 1000 impressions, which means you have to pay $5 every time when your ad hits 1000 impressions. According to these stats, with a budget of $50, you will get 10,000 impressions. It means $50, and your ad can be seen 10,000 times by Amazon users. Isn’t it amazing?

Types of Audience Targeting in Amazon CPM Ads

Advertisements help us attract our dream customers, but unnecessary ads annoy customers as well. But there is no need to worry: With the magic of Amazon CPM Ads, one can target the audience based on their interest, demographics, and behaviours.

Interest-Based Targeting

As the name indicates, you are targeting the audience based on their interests. In interest-based targeting, the seller studies the customer’s browsing history, past shopping experience, and engagement with other categories. Based on these stats, an interest-based chart is created for that specific customer, and ads are shown to them according to their interest.

For instance, if a user’s browser history is based on electronics products, Amazon pits him in the Electronics interested category and deals accordingly. With this method, we can see increased relevance, improvisation in conversational rates, higher engagement, ad visibility, and better ROI.

Behavioral Targeting

The same goes with behavioural targeting; with the past behaviours of the customers, Amazon reads with customers’ behaviour. In the CPM advertising model, Amazon identifies the targeted audience and actively engages with the intent of purchase and sale. This behavioural targeting enhances the conversational rates and tailors the improvised ad efficiencies for better customer insight.

Contextual Targeting

Contextual targeting is based on the context that the customer is watching. In this kind of targeting, the behaviour or interest of the customer isn’t solely based on the previous browsing. However, the interests of the customer are being judged on the basis of the content they are currently engaged with.

Amazon uses a combination of product information, keywords, and page content to place relevant ads. Contextual targeting targets the audience at the moment they are most likely to be responsive.

Retargeting

Suppose you are about to purchase something but, due to some reasons, couldn’t complete your purchase. After some time, the same purchase or things relevant to that purchase came to you. Have you ever wondered why this happens? This is what we call retargeting or remarketing. A strategy of targeting the audience who are about to purchase something so that they will complete their shopping.

The ads act as a stimulus that forces you to fill your abandoned carts. This is also called flexible targeting, which builds trust and improves the brand’s recall.

Best Practices for Targeting the Right Audience

Some of the best practices for targeting of the right audience include a combination of traditional and contemporary methods. Continuous monitoring with regular optimization refines the targeting audience practice.

Another amazing practice to observe and adopt is targeting the competitor’s customer attitude, which will give an insight into the analytics of the better performance of the audience. After understanding the analytics test, experiment with different ad types and analyze what acts best for you.

Limitations & How to Overcome Them

We ensure that the Amazon CPM ad is a powerful method to target the right audience and get the desired results, but with some limitations. The following are some of their solutions’ limitations.

  1. Over-targeting & under-targeting: Over-targeting and under-targeting are two extreme situations that can lead to low engagement, poor click-through rates, or missed opportunities. The remedy to overcome this issue is to refine the targeting data or layering means the combination of multiple strategies for better ROI.
  2. Limited audience data access: While studying the stats of Amazon, another relevant issue that was seen is limited audience data access. In this situation, some policies limit the amount of detailed audience data available to the advertisers. This issue can be resolved with the help of retargeting.
  3. Ad fatigue: Another limitation that advertisers face is ad fatigue. It is the kind of situation that happens when customers are exposed to the same ad numerous times and get annoyed. This issue can be resolved by promoting relevant or creative content and adjusting the frequency cap accordingly.
  4. Ad attribution & tracking challenges: Ad attribution and tracking challenges are another challenge that we have to face, and the best practice to overcome it is to evaluate the performance holistically by combining data from different touch points.

Conclusion

In digital advertising, the CPM model is an exception for now. It is a very cost-effective advertising method that helps you target your prolonged audience with flexibility. By displaying this dynamic method, several ad-based targeting adheres the brands with greater and optimized campaigns to maximise the efficiency of their budget.

The CPM ad, with its impressionist technique, helps us to achieve several things. We would appreciate it if you shared your experience with us in the comment section below. So we all can learn and grow together.

How to Sell Internationally with Amazon Global Selling | SellMora

An in-depth guide to selling internationally with Amazon Global Selling.

You want to expand your business and promote your culture and products globally but don’t know what global sellers are. Wait, this needs to be handled with the care and dedication that SellMora will provide you. We know that Amazon and globalization go hand in hand. So, why not try it out?

This blog will provide you with an in-depth guide to how you can sell internationally with Amazon Global Selling: what you need to sell, where you need to sell, how to sell, what precautions you need to take, what kind of advantages Amazon will provide you in your journey, and many more.

What is Global Sellers?

Let’s start with the basics. Global selling is the game-changer technique for your business, allowing you to enter a new regime of learning and earning internationally. If you are an international seller, you can sell your product worldwide in different regions; Amazon Global Selling will fulfill your wish.

What is Amazon Global Selling?

AGS is an initiative through which sellers can sell their products online globally on Amazon’s global marketplace. Currently operates almost 15+ online marketplaces, providing a breakthrough to online sellers.

Amazon marketplace list you need to be aware of includes:

  • Europe: Germany, United Kingdom, France, Spain, Italy
  • Asia: India, Japan, China, Australia
  • North America: Mexico, Canada, United States, Brazil
  • Middle East: UAE, Turkey

What to Sell in the Amazon Global Market?

The most crucial thing to decide is what to sell globally on Amazon. Some categories that will excel in the global market are furniture, kitchenware, fashion products, beauty products including makeup and skincare, luggage, health and wellness products, office appliances, home appliances, books, jewelry, toys and games, kids, and many more.

Pro Tip: If you want to blow sales, your product needs to be unique and of the best quality; it must be necessary for daily use, and try to add a touch of your culture to your product.

Process of Registration for Amazon Global Selling:

The registration process will follow, and you will decide what you want to sell. You need to follow some simple steps.

1. Marketplace Hunting:

Start exploring the Amazon marketplace and create a selling account. Choose your marketplace carefully after doing the product and business analysis. You can choose any marketplaces mentioned above, covering almost 220 countries worldwide.

Region understanding is a must if you want to expand your business internationally. For instance, if you’re going to sell warm clothes, you must cover cold regions.

2. Global Seller Account Registration:

For Amazon’s global expansion, register your account and verify the details. You need to share information like business details, whether private or any partnerships, location, bank details, seller’s information, ID card for identification purposes, and your credit or debit card details. You also need to verify your documents.

Once you finish the registration process, you must purchase the monthly subscription for your global selling account. The subscription price varies for each marketplace.

  • The Individual selling plan costs $0.99 per sale.
  • The Professional selling plan costs $39.99 per month.

Pro Tip: For instance, in Europe, the individual plan starts at £0.75 per item sold, but North America’s professional plan starts at almost $40 per month.

3. Product Listing:

This is the third step; till now, you are done with the market research and account registration. Now, you must choose your product category and enlist products or items you want to sell.

Add all the details related to your product. One can even change this after some time. You can choose listing tools and templates as well. Another necessary thing to mention is the Universal Product Codes (UCPs).

4. Product Pricing:

Here comes the most crucial yet usherable part of global selling. One needs to be very keen on this section. Before adding price details, one must do a market survey and evaluate the competitors. Then, very precisely, the products should be priced in a way that is cost-effective and cost-friendly not only for the sellers but also for the buyers.

Price your products according to market demand. Focus on the featured section. You can change the prices and add a sale section. Utilize the festivals. While adding the price section, remember the taxes, delivery charges, and logistics charges. They vary in every region.

5. Delivery Procedures:

Now, the time comes when you are finally delivering your product and waiting for their precious reviews. Add the details about delivery. You can offer your products yourself or take advantage of the accommodation Amazon provides.

Amazon Global prioritizes shipping reviews, so let Amazon FBA handle the shipping process for smooth and faster delivery.

Pro Tip: Fulfillment by Amazon is another precious gift Amazon gives its sellers. This service, Amazon, will provide you with an international service to fulfill your orders. Your stocks were sent to the nearest FBA center, and after receiving a request, the product is picked, packed, and shipped within two days (We are not joking it, what Amazon promises and fulfills).

6. Product Promotion:

This is one of the last steps in selling globally on Amazon. You are done with the market research, product analysis, pricing, and registering. It’s time to promote your product so that you can excel in the business.

One needs to analyze the product again, then the marketplace, and then devise strategies for the product’s advertisement. For this purpose, you can join hands with Amazon’s advertising feature, which will help to reach out to broader and demating buyers.

Pro Tip: Understanding country-specific regulations for ultimate and faster growth is best.

Advantages Of Amazon Global Selling:

First, let us share some of the advantages of Amazon Global Selling. Below have a look and appreciate your decision to sell globally on Amazon.

  • More customers = More sales = More money.
  • You don’t need to build a brand. Amazon is providing the marketplace, lessening your branding costs.
  • You and your region will get global recognition.
  • An opportunity to build trust and new strategies.
  • A customer base that is much greater than you can even imagine.
  • A hub of opportunities and collaborations.
  • An ample amount of assistance and experience through Amazon.
  • You are not only earning in several currencies but there is also an increase in sales during different festivals.

Precautions in Global Selling:

We will never discuss the disadvantages of global selling or Amazon global selling because it is inappropriate in many ways. Instead, we want you to take precautions when entering the Amazon global selling regime.

  • Always choose your best seller to promote globally; otherwise, it will affect your business locally.
  • Be ready because the competition is wild; everyone wants to excel, so decide wisely.
  • You need to cater to severe marketing strategies for different countries.
  • Choose your people wisely because there would be a bulk of things to handle.
  • Another hurdle you will face while spanning your business is the reviews for your product.
  • There will be hurdles like language barriers, pricing strategy, tax issues, customer service complications, etc.

Pro Tip: Your time is precious, so you must choose your team wisely, and SellMora is the best option.

Conclusion:

Amazon itself states that you can accelerate your sales with Amazon’s global brand, simplify global expansion, and grow and diversify your sales internationally. If you prioritize Amazon’s global strategy, your sales will be boosted.

Amazon gives you flexibility, customer clearance, tracking shipments, end-to-end Amazon global logistics, cross-border shipments, and many more. Still waiting, go and start your journey with Amazon Global Selling.
